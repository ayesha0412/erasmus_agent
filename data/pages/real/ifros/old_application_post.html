Application for the new intake 2022-2024 is now open

Posted on 2 de November de 202129 de November de 2022 By Masterifros

It is with pleasure that we announce the opening of the next application for the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Intelligent Field Robotic Systems for the period 2022-2024.

Interested in applying for the Erasmus Mundus scholarship? You can submit your application until 14th February, 2022.

Interested in applying as a self-funded student? You can submit your application until 14th May, 2022.
